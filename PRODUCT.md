## Product Principles

1. Every line on the page is traceable to the LinkedIn PDF. Unknowns are labeled placeholders, never guesses.
2. The recruiter's minute comes first: name, current role, the four-industry arc, and contact must land in the first viewport.
3. Show the seam between engineering and business as the story, not a list of jobs.
4. Contact is one click away from every section.
5. Hand-over friendly: one folder, readable HTML, no build step, so Bennett can edit text himself.
